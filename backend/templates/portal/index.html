Collect premiums. Settle claims. Built for Africa.

TrustLayer helps insurance organisations collect member premiums via M-Pesa, hold funds securely, and settle claims — all without building payment infrastructure.

How it works

💳

Generate QR codes

Create payment links and QR codes instantly. Members scan and pay via M-Pesa.

🛡️

Funds held securely

Premiums are collected into TrustLayer's secure ledger — not released until you approve.

💰

Settle to your account

Withdraw collected premiums to your M-Pesa or bank account on your settlement schedule.

📊

Real-time dashboard

Track collections, pending settlements, and transaction history in one place.
