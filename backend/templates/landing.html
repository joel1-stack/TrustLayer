How it works

The loop, end to end

Eight steps from the first report to the closed, customer-verified record. No status page. No support ticket. A real loop.

1

Report

Customer submits the problem. Gets a case ID instantly.

2

Evidence

Screenshots attached. Case moves to evidence collection.

3

Route

Assigned to the right team. Priority set automatically.

4

Investigate

Agent reviews full case, adds notes, messages the customer.

5

Resolve

Agent records the resolution: fraud, legitimate, error.

6

Verify

Customer confirms: resolved or reopened. One tap.

7

Closed

Full immutable record of every step and decision.

8

Reopened

If the customer says no, the case goes back to investigation.

Under the hood

Five deterministic engines

No AI. No ML. Just rules, checklists, and a state machine. Every decision is auditable and explainable.

Evidence Engine

Checklist per case type. Shows customer what's missing. Shows agent what's complete. Drives completeness score.

Routing Engine

Assigns team based on case type. Sets priority by amount. One lookup table, fully auditable.

SLA Engine

Per-type deadlines. Warning at 80%. Breach at 100%. Countdown on every agent screen.

Notification Engine

Event triggers SMS, email, or in-app alert. Customer and agent always informed. One-way event feed.

Audit Engine

Every action logged. Append-only. Nobody edits, nobody deletes. The product's trustworthiness.

State Machine

9 states. Clear transitions. No case gets stuck. Every move recorded. Customer closes the loop.
