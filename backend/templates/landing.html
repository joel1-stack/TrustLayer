Core Engines

Modular. Testable. Replaceable.

🔗

Context Engine

Assembles operational context from enterprise adapters — subscriber, billing, network, device state.

🔍

Diagnosis Engine

Identifies root cause from assembled context using rule-based and ML-powered analysis.

⚖️

Policy Engine

Authorizes actions based on rules, risk level, and clearance requirements. AI does not have authority.

⚡

Action Engine

Executes authorized remediation actions across enterprise systems with full audit trail.

✅

Verification Engine

Confirms service recovery and validates that actions produced the expected outcome.

📋

Truth Engine

Immutable audit trail with SHA-256 chains. Every action, every decision, every outcome recorded.
