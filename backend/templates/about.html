About

TrustLayer

A verification loop for customer problems. Not a bank ledger. Not a support ticket system. A loop that closes.

What we built

TrustLayer takes one customer problem, gathers the evidence around it, gets it to the right person, and produces a verified resolution. That's it.

What we didn't build

No multi-org platform. No five integration engines. No telecom adapters. No escrow. No policy engine. No diagnosis engine. Just the loop.

The problem

When a customer reports a transaction dispute, they get a ticket number and silence. No timeline. No evidence collection. No verification. The case dies in a queue.

The solution

A closed loop: report → evidence → route → investigate → resolve → verify → closed. Every step recorded. Every transition logged. Customer confirms the outcome. No dead ends.

Built in Kenya

Django, PostgreSQL, Redis. Docker. One server. Two browsers. Customer and agent. That's the whole stack.
