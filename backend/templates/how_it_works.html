How it works

The loop, end to end

Eight steps from the first report to the closed, customer-verified record. No status page. No support ticket. A real loop.

1

Report

Customer submits the problem. Gets a case ID instantly. No wait time.

2

Evidence

Screenshots attached. Case moves to evidence collection automatically.

3

Route

Assigned to the right team. Priority set automatically based on amount.

4

Investigate

Agent reviews full case. Adds notes. Messages the customer directly.

5

Resolve

Agent records the resolution: fraud, legitimate, error, or unable to determine.

6

Verify

Customer confirms: resolved or reopened. One tap. No phone call needed.

7

Closed

Full immutable record of every step and decision. Timestamped.

8

Reopened?

If the customer says no, the case goes back to investigation. No dead ends.
