How TrustLayer Works

Six steps from problem to verified resolution. Complete visibility at every stage. No black boxes, no mystery queues, no lost tickets.

1

You Submit a Report

Describe the problem, provide transaction details, upload evidence (screenshots, receipts, emails). Our form structures your case so nothing gets lost in translation.

You get a unique Case ID (e.g., CASE-4721) immediately
Case is tagged with problem type, priority, and routing metadata
All evidence is stored securely and attached to your case
2

We Route It to the Right Team

Based on case type (fraud, payment dispute, account issue), priority (urgent, high, normal), and merchant metadata, the case is assigned to a specialized investigation team.

Teams are staffed by issue type (Fraud, Payments, Identity, Account)
Routing happens instantly — no manual triage delays
Timeline shows "Assigned to [Team Name]" with timestamp
3

Agent Opens Investigation

An agent picks up your case from their queue. Status changes to "Under Investigation." Timeline and messaging unlock so you can see every update and communicate directly.

You see agent's name and can send messages in real-time
Agent can request additional evidence or clarification
Every action appears on the timeline immediately
4

Evidence Review & Merchant Contact

Agent reviews all evidence, contacts the merchant or payment processor, and gathers additional data. This stage can take 24-72 hours depending on case complexity.

Agent logs internal notes visible only to the team
Customer sees updates like "Merchant contacted" or "Evidence reviewed"
Estimated resolution time is displayed and updated
5

Agent Records Resolution

Agent documents the outcome (refund processed, dispute won, account corrected, etc.), includes proof or reference numbers, and marks case as "Pending Verification."

Resolution notes are visible to the customer
Customer receives email/SMS with verification link
Case does not close until customer verifies
6

You Verify the Resolution

You confirm the problem is actually solved. Click "Yes, Resolved" to close the case, or "No, Still an Issue" to reopen investigation. No resolution without verification.

Verified cases are permanently marked "Resolved" in your history
Reopened cases go back to the same agent with your feedback
You can leave feedback and see aggregate resolution stats

Our Core Principles

Every design decision is built on these four pillars.

Complete Visibility

You see every event, every message, every status change. No hidden steps, no mystery queues.

Real-Time Updates

Timeline and messages update instantly. No refresh required, no delayed notifications.

Verified Outcomes

Cases don't close until you confirm the problem is solved. Your verification is required.

Secure & Private

All evidence is encrypted at rest and in transit. Only you and assigned agents see your case.
