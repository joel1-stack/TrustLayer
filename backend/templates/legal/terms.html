TRUSTLAYER

Escrow Terms of Service

Last updated: May 2026 · Applies to all TrustLayer escrow transactions

1. What TrustLayer Does

TrustLayer is an escrow orchestration service. When you pay via TrustLayer, your money is held securely and only released to the seller after you confirm you have received what you paid for.

2. How Funds Are Held

During the MVP phase, funds are received via M-Pesa into a dedicated TrustLayer business paybill. Funds are reconciled daily and released only upon confirmed delivery. TrustLayer does not hold funds indefinitely.

3. Buyer Obligations

  • You must confirm delivery within 48 hours of receiving the item or service.
  • If you do not confirm within 48 hours after the seller confirms delivery, funds may be auto-released.
  • You must raise disputes within 24 hours of the delivery deadline.

4. Seller Obligations

  • You must deliver the item or service as described in the deal.
  • You must confirm delivery once the item has been delivered.
  • Misrepresentation of goods or services is grounds for refund and account suspension.

5. Fees

TrustLayer charges a service fee of 1.5% of the transaction amount, split between buyer and seller. The fee is shown clearly before payment is initiated.

6. Disputes

If a dispute is raised, TrustLayer will review evidence from both parties and make a binding decision within 48 hours. See our Dispute Resolution Policy for full details.

7. Refunds

Refunds are issued when: (a) the seller fails to deliver, (b) the item is materially different from the description, or (c) both parties agree to cancel. Refunds are processed within 24 hours of approval.

8. Limitation of Liability

TrustLayer is not liable for losses arising from buyer or seller misrepresentation, M-Pesa service outages, or force majeure events. Our liability is limited to the transaction amount held.

9. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya. Disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of Kenyan courts.
