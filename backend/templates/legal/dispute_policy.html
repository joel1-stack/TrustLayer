Dispute Resolution Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · The 10% Rule — financial consequences for bad faith

1. How to Raise a Dispute

Either party can raise a dispute by contacting TrustLayer support at support@trustlayer.app with the deal code (TL-XXXXXX) and evidence. Disputes must be raised within 24 hours of the delivery deadline.

2. The 10% Rule

Bad Faith Penalty

If a party raises a dispute that is found to be in bad faith (e.g., false claims, fabricated evidence), they forfeit 10% of the transaction amount as a penalty. This is deducted before any refund or release.

Refusal to Cooperate

If a seller refuses to respond to a legitimate dispute within 24 hours, the full amount is refunded to the buyer. If a buyer refuses to confirm delivery after confirmed receipt, funds are auto-released to the seller after 48 hours.

3. Evidence Required

  • Photos or videos of the item at delivery
  • WhatsApp or SMS communication between parties
  • M-Pesa transaction receipts
  • Any written agreement or listing description

4. Resolution Timeline

  • Dispute raised → TrustLayer acknowledges within 2 hours
  • Evidence review → 24 hours
  • Decision issued → 48 hours from dispute raised
  • Funds released or refunded → within 24 hours of decision

5. Decision is Final

TrustLayer's dispute decision is final and binding. By using TrustLayer, both parties agree to accept the outcome of the dispute resolution process.

6. Fraud and Misrepresentation

Any party found to have committed fraud, identity theft, or deliberate misrepresentation will have their account permanently suspended and may be reported to relevant authorities.
